# Licensing gaps

Which controls actually cover which users. Real compliance programmes get this
wrong constantly, usually by assuming a tenant-wide policy is tenant-wide in
*effect*.

Your tenant is deliberately mixed: E5 on the admin account, Business Standard on
Barry, a guest with no licence at all. Log every place a control covers one and
not another.
